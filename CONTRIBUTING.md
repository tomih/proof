# Contributing

PROOF is open to criticism and improvement.

A useful contribution states:

- the version and requirement identifiers affected;
- the concrete ambiguity, contradiction, missing control case, or fixture failure;
- the proposed change;
- whether the proposal changes an obligation or only clarifies it;
- the evidence or synthetic case that demonstrates the issue.

Do not submit confidential, personal, client, production, credential, or regulated information. Use synthetic fixtures unless you have clear authority to publish the source material.

By contributing material for inclusion, you agree that accepted contributions may be released under the same CC BY 4.0 license as PROOF. This release uses one license; contributions requiring a different or additional license cannot be accepted into it.

Accepted contributions become part of a PROOF release only when approved under `GOVERNANCE.md`.
